## My Favorite Movie: Spirited Away 

### Summary

*Spirited Away*, directed by Hayao Miyazaki and produced by Studio Ghibli, is an animated film released in 2001. It tells the story of Chihiro, a young girl who enters a magical world and must work at a bathhouse for spirits to free herself and her parents, who have been turned into pigs.

### Main Characters

-   Chihiro Ogino : The protagonist, a brave and kind girl.
-   Haku : A mysterious boy who can transform into a dragon and helps Chihiro.
-   Yubaba : The powerful witch who runs the bathhouse.
-   No-Face : A lonely spirit who becomes fascinated by Chihiro.
